# Mission Meow
In Mission Meow you have to find your chaotic cat, Sir Wobble.
But watch out, he stinks! Therefore, you can´t touch him but have to guide him with a laser pointer instead.
Navigate through various puzzles in order to progress through diverse rooms with the goal to clean Sir Wobble and finally guide him to his well deserved bowl of food.

Have fun!
